What is Billing Data Submission

One platform for all billing and expense workflows

How Billing Data Submission works

From invoice submission to payment release

Every transaction follows a structured lifecycle: submission → verification → approval → finance review → payment release → audit trail.

010203040506

01 · Submit

Vendors upload invoices via the portal; internal teams record expenses.

02 · Verify

Procurement and supply chain verify invoices against purchase orders and delivery records.

03 · Approve

Department heads and managers review submissions through defined approval routes.

04 · Finance Review

Finance reviews submissions, budget alignment and payment preparation.

05 · Release

Payments are coordinated and released through integrated finance workflows.

06 · Audit Trail

Submissions, approvals, rejections and payments are logged.
